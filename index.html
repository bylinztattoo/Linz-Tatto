Meet the Artist

I’m Ailin, a Cuban tattoo artist based in Savannah, Georgia, currently creating at the renowned Tramp Art Studios.

Formally trained at the National Academy of Fine Arts in Havana, Cuba, I bring a strong foundation in fine arts and a meticulous attention to detail into every piece I create.

My work specializes in fine line, micro realism, black & grey, color, blackwork, and line-dot techniques — blending precision, softness, and composition to create custom tattoos that feel both refined and timeless.

I’m especially drawn to delicate, highly detailed designs inspired by color elements, animals, transparency effects, and organic textures. Each piece is thoughtfully designed to complement the body while maintaining individuality and artistic intention.

For me, tattooing is more than applying ink to skin — it is a collaborative artistic experience built on trust, creativity, and craftsmanship.

My goal is to create meaningful pieces that not only look beautiful today, but continue to age with elegance over time.

Let’s create something timeless together.

Booking Policy

Before You Book

A non-refundable $100 deposit is required to secure your appointment. The deposit will be applied toward the final cost of your tattoo.

Once availability is confirmed and we’ve discussed your idea, a payment link will be sent to finalize your booking. You may also place your deposit over the phone using a card during business hours.

A minimum of 48 hours notice is required to reschedule. With proper notice, your deposit may be transferred to a new date or placed on a “To Be Determined” (TBD) list.

Deposits on the TBD list remain valid for up to one year from the original appointment date.

Your tattoo is an open wound and proper aftercare is essential to ensure the best healed result. Please follow these instructions carefully throughout the healing process.

  • Wash your tattoo gently 2–3 times daily using lukewarm water and mild, fragrance-free soap during the first week.
  • While showering, avoid direct high-pressure water on the tattooed area. Pat dry gently with a clean paper towel — never rub the skin.
  • Begin applying the aftercare product recommended by your tattoo artist starting on the third day. Apply only a very thin layer after each wash.
  • Do not use petroleum jelly or any products that were not recommended by your tattoo artist.
  • Do not re-wrap or cover the tattoo unless specifically instructed to do so.
  • Avoid touching the tattoo with unwashed hands. Do not scratch, peel, or pick at scabs or flaking skin, as this may affect healing and damage the final result.
  • Avoid direct sun exposure until the tattoo is fully healed. After healing, always use sunscreen to help preserve the quality and longevity of your tattoo.
  • Do not swim or soak the tattoo in pools, hot tubs, lakes, rivers, or the ocean during the healing process.
  • Wear loose, clean clothing to avoid friction and prevent fabric from sticking to the tattoo.
  • Avoid excessive sweating, intense physical activity, alcohol consumption, and close contact with animals during the first week of healing.
  • Healing times may vary depending on placement, skin type, and lifestyle. If you experience excessive redness, swelling, unusual pain, or any signs of infection, seek medical attention immediately.

Following these aftercare instructions will help your tattoo heal properly and maintain the best possible detail, contrast, and longevity.

Each tattoo is a custom piece of art, and pricing is based on several factors to ensure fair value for both the design and the detail involved.

My rates are determined by:

  • Size of the tattoo
  • Level of detail and complexity
  • Style of the piece, such as fine line, realism, black & grey, color, and more
  • Placement on the body
  • Whether the design is fully custom or based on a concept

All tattoos are priced per piece, not by generic estimates, to ensure clarity and fairness for each project.

Because every tattoo is unique, I provide a final quote once I have reviewed your idea, references, and placement.

For larger or more detailed projects, pricing may be broken down into sessions depending on the scale of the work.

If you’re unsure about budget, feel free to reach out — I’ll always guide you toward the best option for your idea.
