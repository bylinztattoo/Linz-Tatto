Booking Policy

Before You Book

A $100 non-refundable deposit is required to book your tattoo appointment. Your deposit will be applied toward the final price of your tattoo on the day of your session.

After your appointment details are confirmed, we will text you a secure link to pay your deposit and finalize your booking. You may also call the shop during business hours to place your deposit over the phone with a card: (912) 349-3195.

If you need to reschedule, a minimum 48-hour notice is required. With proper notice, your appointment can be rescheduled or your deposit can be placed on a “To Be Determined” list. Deposits on the TBD list are saved for up to one year from your original appointment date.

Please bring a valid photo ID showing that you are 18 years of age or older. Accepted forms of ID include a driver’s license, passport, carry license, or other valid government-issued photo identification. No one under the age of 18 is allowed inside the studio.
